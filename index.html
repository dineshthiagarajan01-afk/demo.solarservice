FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Is the rooftop survey really free?

Yes. Our solar experts visit your property, assess your rooftop and usage, and recommend a system at no cost or obligation.

Will you help with subsidy paperwork?

Yes — we collect the required documents and handle subsidy and bank-process documentation on your behalf.

How long does installation take?

Timelines vary with system size and site, but most residential systems are installed and tested within a few days.

What happens after installation?

We hand over the completed project and walk you through the system, then provide ongoing service and maintenance support.
